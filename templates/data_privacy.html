Data Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, the rights you have over the personal data Ateker Music holds about you, in addition to what's described in our Privacy Policy.

1. Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of your account and associated data, and request a copy of your data in a portable format.

2. How to Exercise These Rights

Most information — your name, bio, avatar, and social links — can be updated directly from your account or artist profile settings. For account deletion or data export requests, reach out through our Contact page and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (hosting your account and, for artists, your uploaded music), to comply with legal obligations, and based on your consent where required, such as when you agree to the Artist Upload Agreement.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Account records are stored in our database, and uploaded audio and images are stored with Cloudflare R2, a cloud object storage provider. Data may be processed in different countries depending on where these providers operate their infrastructure.

5. Children's Data

Ateker Music is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone below the age required to create an account in their country.

6. Complaints

If you believe your data has been mishandled, you can contact us directly, and, where applicable, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
