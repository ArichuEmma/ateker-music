Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text). Artists additionally provide a stage name, bio, and optional social links. We also automatically collect basic usage data such as pages visited and songs played or downloaded, to keep the platform running and to improve it.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, display artist profiles and songs publicly, send account-related emails (such as verification and password resets), moderate uploaded content, and understand overall platform usage.

3. What We Share Publicly

Your stage name, bio, avatar, social links, and uploaded songs are visible to all visitors once approved. Your email address and account password are never shown publicly.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare R2 for audio and image storage, and an email provider to send account notifications. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to remember basic preferences. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights

You can review and update your account information at any time from your profile settings, and can request deletion of your account and data by contacting us. See our Data Privacy page for more detail on your rights.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
