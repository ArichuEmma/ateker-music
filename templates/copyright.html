Copyright Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Respecting Copyright

Ateker Music respects the intellectual property rights of artists and third parties. Artists who upload music must own the rights to what they upload, or have explicit permission from the rights holder, including for any samples, beats, or features used.

2. Artist Responsibility

By uploading a song, an artist confirms — as part of the Artist Upload Agreement — that the content does not infringe on anyone else's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights.

3. Reporting a Copyright Concern

If you believe a song on Ateker Music infringes your copyright, please contact us through our Contact page with: (1) a description of the copyrighted work, (2) a link to the song on our platform, (3) your contact information, and (4) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized.

4. Our Response

We review copyright reports and may remove or restrict access to the reported song while we investigate. Repeat or confirmed infringement can result in permanent removal of the content and suspension of the artist's account.

5. Counter-Notice

An artist whose song was removed following a copyright report may contact us to provide evidence of ownership or permission, and request that the song be reinstated.

6. Repeat Infringers

Artist accounts found to repeatedly upload infringing content will be suspended or permanently removed from the platform.
